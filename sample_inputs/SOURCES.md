# Public completed tax-form samples

The numbered PDF files below are publisher-provided examples, downloaded unchanged for local testing. They are separate examples; do not treat them as a single taxpayer’s return or submit them to a tax authority.

| File | Publisher and original |
|---|---|
| `11_sample_w2_pitt_payroll.pdf` | [University of Pittsburgh Payroll — Sample W-2](https://www.payroll.pitt.edu/sites/default/files/assets/Sample-W2.pdf) |
| `12_sample_1099_nec_taxbit.pdf` | [Taxbit — Sample U.S. Form 1099-NEC](https://info.taxbit.com/hubfs/SAMPLE-US_2026_1099NEC.pdf?hsLang=en) |
| `13_sample_1099_k_taxbit.pdf` | [Taxbit — Sample U.S. Form 1099-K](https://info.taxbit.com/hubfs/SAMPLE-US_2026_1099K.pdf?hsLang=en) |

Files `01`–`10` are intake examples. A mention of a tax form in an intake document does not establish any box value.
